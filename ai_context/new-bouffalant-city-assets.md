# New Bouffalant City Environment Assets

Imported runtime art lives at [`../art/environments/new_bouffalant_city/`](../art/environments/new_bouffalant_city/).
Reusable terrain and structure scenes live under
[`../game/world/level_kits/`](../game/world/level_kits/), while the playable
city and its interiors live under
[`../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/`](../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/).
The art pack's local [`README.md`](../art/environments/new_bouffalant_city/README.md)
is the asset usage guide.

## Asset Families

- [`ground/`](../game/world/level_kits/terrain/new_bouffalant_city/ground/) is the canonical 2 × 2 m city-cobble and grass/dirt kit. Its shared `ground_tile_mesh_library.tres` preserves cobble IDs 0–5 and assigns grass/dirt IDs 6–11 with the same identity transform, shadow, collision, and navigation contract. Eleven directly placeable wrappers live under `ground/scenes/`. The common `level_base.tscn` provides an empty grid at the origin; New Bouffalant City overrides that inherited grid at `(3, 0, -1)` with its 270 painted cells.
- [`route/`](../game/world/level_kits/terrain/new_bouffalant_city/route/) is the six-piece 4 × 4 m meadow/dirt kit. Place these as snapped scene instances rather than adding them to the 2 × 2 m GridMap library. Each scene has a centered surface pivot and a 4 × 0.25 × 4 m static collision slab.
- [`route_00.tscn`](../game/world/levels/standalone_areas/routes/route_00/route_00.tscn) is the first full outdoor-route consumer and the authored reference for the 40 standalone routes: an 8 × 12 layout of 96 unit-scale route modules with 21 dirt-path modules, five reusable tall-grass encounter fields, an authored navigation region, seven standard trainers in Lv. 3–6 order, mandatory sight chokepoints, a far-end progression gate, and unit-scale vegetation from `reference_city_pack/models/`. Its red return object targets the Gate Building rear room.
- `reference_city_pack/models/` contains 147 grounded GLBs and 1,831 companion PNGs produced by Godot's Extract Textures import mode. The original image payloads are embedded in the GLBs, but the complete imported directory, including `.glb.import` scale and collision configuration, is the transferable unit.
- [`tools/new_bouffalant_city_import/`](../tools/new_bouffalant_city_import/) owns the selective collision profiles, `EditorScenePostImport` generator, and model-import settings tool used by every imported GLB.
- `reference_city_pack/catalog.json` indexes 164 assets: the 147 GLBs, six route tiles, five canonical cobble wrappers, and six grass/dirt wrappers.
- [`building_ground_metric_showcase.tscn`](../tests/manual/new_bouffalant_city/building_ground_metric_showcase.tscn) is the complete player-calibrated browser and scale-reference scene.
- [`pokemon_center_with_roof.tscn`](../game/world/level_kits/structures/pokemon_center/pokemon_center_with_roof.tscn) is the fitted reusable Pokemon Center assembly used by the canonical city. It preserves the unchanged building import and baked `0.75` scale/collision under a unit-scale wrapper, then adds a 201-triangle roof and two street-facing automatic door sets sharing one 56-triangle mesh. The two additions use one opaque palette material each and have no collision.
- [`pokemon_center_interior.tscn`](../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/interiors/pokemon_center/pokemon_center_interior.tscn) is the playable five-sided main clinic. It combines a 1,948-triangle, two-surface opaque environment with simplified authored collision, the common level hierarchy, a staffed healer, and entry/exit/service markers.
- [`pokemon_center_annex.tscn`](../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/interiors/pokemon_center/pokemon_center_annex.tscn) is the distinct east-storefront service interior. It combines a 1,962-triangle, two-surface opaque environment with 13 authored collision shapes, the common level hierarchy, and its own entry/exit/service markers.
- [`interiors/`](../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/interiors/) retains the other eight city-connected scenes: City Hall, Rouge Tower, Miare Station, the garage, Gate Building, both tenant instances, and the museum. They inherit the canonical level base directly and share reusable room, door, and lighting scenes. Miare Station owns the ordinary Stretchman resident instance, and the Gate Building's rear room owns the walk-through Route 0 door and safe return marker.

## Editor Placement Workflow

The enabled editor plugin at [`../addons/new_bouffalant_city_asset_palette/`](../addons/new_bouffalant_city_asset_palette/) reads `catalog.json` and exposes all entries as bundled thumbnail cards through the **Bouffalant Assets** dock. It places undoable `PackedScene` instances under a `NewBouffalantCityAssets` child of the edited scene. Placement supports search/category filtering, 0.5/2/4 m XZ snap, 90-degree rotation, upward-facing collision surfaces, and a fallback Y plane. The plugin's [`README.md`](../addons/new_bouffalant_city_asset_palette/README.md) is the operator guide and records the thumbnail regeneration command.

This dock complements rather than replaces `ModularGroundGrid`: use the shared GridMap MeshLibrary to paint strict 2 × 2 m cobble or grass/dirt cells, and use the dock for their direct wrappers, 4 × 4 m route scenes, and irregular or unique assets. Do not make one GridMap library from the full catalog.

### Vulkan-safe targeted mesh imports

`runtime_contract.gd` classifies complete environment sections, assets with at least 25,000 triangles, and assets with a runtime horizontal extent of at least 60 m as performance-sensitive. There are currently 44; this classification is advisory and does not block placement or play.

Museum (`t1_b_museum`) and Gate Building (`t1_b_gate_building`) previously reproduced a Linux Intel `i915` GPU hang and context reset under Vulkan/Forward+, followed by Godot aborting after device loss. This was not a GDScript exception, physics crash, corrupt source GLB, general polygon-count limit, or texture/material failure: source positions, normals, UVs, tangents, base indices, generated LOD indices, and textures validated; flat materials did not eliminate the reset; and larger City Hall, Rouge Tower, and Miare Station controls rendered normally.

The asset-level solution is to keep the original imported mesh buffers for Gate Building, Museum, and Tenant Building (`t1_b_tenant_building`). For only these three IDs, `meshes/generate_lods=false` and `meshes/create_shadow_meshes=false`. Removing both optional Godot-generated buffer types avoids the Intel Vulkan driver path that hung while retaining the source geometry, original per-surface textured materials, baked `0.75` scale, generated gameplay collision, and normal shadow casting through the original mesh. Gate and Museum were the reproduced failures; Tenant uses the same targeted profile preventively, not because a Tenant-only hang was independently confirmed.

[`runtime_contract.gd`](../game/world/level_kits/structures/new_bouffalant_city/runtime_contract.gd)
owns the three-ID `VULKAN_SAFE_MESH_IMPORT_IDS` set.
[`apply_model_import_settings.gd`](../tools/new_bouffalant_city_import/apply_model_import_settings.gd)
enforces both settings as `false` for that set and `true` for every other GLB.
Keep the exception centralized there; do not hand-edit an imported cache,
globally disable the optimizations, flatten these assets' materials, or restore
the old Compatibility-restart/placement guard. The palette permits ordinary
double-click placement and ordinary play on Forward+.

After the targeted reimport, an animated directional-shadow stress scene containing all three affected imports plus the three controls completed 600 frames on Intel ADL GT2 Vulkan/Forward+. The real [`../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/new_bouffalant_city.tscn`](../game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/new_bouffalant_city.tscn) also completed 600 iterations on the same Intel Forward+ path. These successful runs are the current regression baseline. The two importer options were disabled together, so documentation must not claim that either LOD generation or shadow-mesh generation was individually isolated as the sole trigger.

## Placement And Classification

Keep runtime instances at unit scale. The 147 GLB imports bake the source geometry to `0.75`, calibrated against the 1.67 m player; do not also scale their scene instances. The catalog preserves source-space GLB bounds and declares `runtime_import_scale`, while the palette and showcase present the resulting runtime bounds. The 17 authored ground scenes are not reduced and retain their exact dimensions. Normal placement uses 0.5 m translation snapping and 90° rotations. Ground pivots are centered on the walkable surface at local `Y = 0`, with their 0.25 m slabs below the surface.

Use `game/world/level_kits/structures/pokemon_center/pokemon_center_with_roof.tscn`
when the fitted roof and doors are required; keep the reference GLB itself unchanged. The roof's recessed drum begins at local `Y = 3.015 m`. Its five-sided eave uses the complete 10.26 m source-model envelope plus a small overhang, sits at `Y = 4.552 m` above the building's `Y = 4.432 m` maximum, and covers roughly 60.1 m² in plan. The broad tiered crown rises to about `Y = 6.139 m`. The automatic door storefronts fill both broad street-facing openings beneath the large red emblems rather than the arched corner window; they run from local `Y = 0.360 m` to `Y = 3.070 m`. Their outer borders deliberately overlap the measured side, sill, and upper-trim boundaries to prevent background seams at oblique camera angles while remaining behind the projecting facade pieces. The door authoring check permits building overlap only for those tagged outer frame layers; glass and hardware intersections still fail generation. Both assets use one opaque 64 × 16 palette material, cast ordinary directional shadows, and omit tangents, generated LODs, separate optimized shadow meshes, animation, runtime generation code, and collision. Roof inputs, the Hunyuan3D-2mv runner, constrained finalizer, door builder, previews, and regeneration notes live under `source_assets/pokemon_center_roof/`.

Use `game/world/levels/new_bouffalant_city/interiors/pokemon_center/pokemon_center_interior.tscn`
as the playable main clinic. Its 12 × 10 m five-sided cutaway footprint echoes the exterior while keeping the foreground low for the lower follow camera. The art direction treats it as a near-future clinic renovated inside an older civic shell: warm worn brick, limestone pilasters/cornices, dark walnut wainscot and seating, and restrained broad grime surround the vivid red/cyan clinic technology. The visual environment remains one unit-scale mesh with two opaque draws: a 128 × 16 palette and repeating 512 × 512 brick albedo. It omits tangents, animation, generated LODs, an optimized shadow mesh, generated triangle collision, and additional material maps. Fourteen authored boxes/cylinders handle static gameplay collision. One directional light provides basic shadows; the two low-energy counter fills remain shadow-free. The formal male `PokemonCenterHealer` reuses the existing waiter art pack at `NurseSpawn`; its Godot import adds 7,656 runtime skinned triangles in nine material draws backed by nine 256 × 256 albedo textures. A player-only sphere reaches across the counter and opens the behavior-owned Heal/Not now UI. The counter and its static box sit 0.35 m farther forward than the original composition, leaving a measured service aisle around the attendant capsule without increasing the environment's triangle, draw, light, or static-collision counts. `EntrySpawn` and two visitor markers provide the remaining placement contracts. `ExitToCity` returns to the safe `PokemonCenterEntrance` marker outside the south facade. Deterministic Blender inputs, editable source, palette, generated brick source/derivative, and preview live under `source_assets/pokemon_center_interior/`.

The city has 10 authored contact-triggered exterior openings. `PokemonCenterSouthEntranceTrigger` targets the main clinic, while `PokemonCenterEastEntranceTrigger` targets the distinct annex service environment. The other eight openings cover three City Hall approaches, the Miare Station double doors, the sole city-facing Gate Building door, two tenant doors, and the museum doors. Rouge Tower and garage transition triggers and return markers are absent. The two tenant instances use distinct scenes so neither can return to the other building. Miare Station instances the ordinary resident `Stretchman`, whose generic `MenuNpcBehavior` opens the Adventure Menu; menu destinations travel through `ChallengeProgressionSystem` and return through their authored gateways. Entering the Gate Building places the player well beyond its narrow front exit threshold. Its illuminated `ROUTE 0` rear door uses contact transfer with no interaction prop, while Route 0 places a red interactive return object beside `Route0Start` that targets the adjacent `Route0ReturnSpawn` in the rear room. The front `CITY EXIT` label and blue floor guide make the way back outside explicit.

The main city scene also owns a seven-member `TownResidents` group. These
characters use non-battling repeatable conversation behavior, three use women
art packs, and three use the bounded local-roaming subclass. Their dialog builds
regional, borough, council, Miare Station, and Team Bastion lore. Researcher
Lumen is fixed to an interior cobblestone plaza tile at `(10, 0, 5)` and grants
the one-time Exp. Share; the focused town test verifies his surface placement
in addition to the roster, models, roaming radii, dialog, and gift. Their shared
`resident_base.tscn` role inherits `game/actors/character/pfr_character.tscn`; each level-authored art
pack override now produces a non-persistent editor preview, so all seven models
remain visible while editing without serializing model children into the city.

Imported building collision remains unchanged and solid. Every exterior transfer box therefore reaches onto the accessible side of its facade or begins at the outer walkable approach when stairs or props occupy the doorway. Contact transfer has no confirmation step, so all eight non-Pokemon-Center boxes use compact opening-width volumes only `0.35 m` deep; do not expand them across stairs, plazas, or sidewalks. Each trigger stores a verified `metadata/approach_position`; every corresponding exterior return marker sits beyond the trigger volume to avoid an arrival loop. The regression test also places the live player `0.9 m` toward each safe exterior return direction and requires no overlap before testing contact at the threshold. The Miare Station trigger at the central double doors is exercised through live `body_entered` physics rather than signal injection alone. All eight live string-addressed destination scenes (the two Pokemon Center scenes plus City Hall, Miare Station, Gate Building, both tenant scenes, and the museum) must remain explicitly selected in the Web export. Annex authoring source and preview live under `source_assets/pokemon_center_annex/`.

Catalog entries under `Complete Environment Sections` are large source-authored assemblies, not modular tiles. Only `Modular Ground` entries follow the strict 2/4/8 m ground system.

Imported GLBs generate layer-1 static collision at import time, after the baked scale is applied. Hard-surface assets use double-sided concave mesh shapes; shrubs and hedges use boxes; trees, bamboo, and stumps use central cylinders. Grass, flowers, loose soil, the fissure decal, and water-only entries intentionally have no collision. The 17 modular ground scenes retain authored slabs. This collision is for static prototype environment use and does not replace per-level navigation, occlusion, or interaction authoring. When adding models, changing profiles, or regenerating `.glb.import` files, run `tools/new_bouffalant_city_import/apply_model_import_settings.gd`, then refresh imports. This step also preserves the three Vulkan-safe mesh exceptions.

## Storage And Provenance

Keep the extracted model PNGs and their `.import` metadata beside the GLBs. Godot materializes those files from the embedded image payloads and the imported scenes reference them. Do not create another duplicate source-texture archive inside the runtime pack. Four additional shared textures under `route_tile/textures/` serve the authored 4 × 4 m route scenes. Six 512 × 512 maps under `ground_tile/textures/` are extracted from the packed grass/dirt Blender source and shared by three opaque materials; both OpenGL normal maps keep `process/normal_map_invert_y=false`.

[`../source_assets/grass_dirt_path_kit_01.blend`](../source_assets/grass_dirt_path_kit_01.blend) remains the unchanged authority for the six 2 × 2 m grass/dirt collections. Conversion exports each collection to an uncompressed temporary GLB, lets Godot bake the Blender-to-Godot axis conversion into vertex arrays, replaces embedded materials with the three shared native materials, and saves only native `ArrayMesh` resources. Do not retain the temporary GLBs.

The reference-city GLBs are derived from Pokémon Z-A field assets. The catalog retains per-asset provenance. Treat them as prototype/reference content unless the project's rights and production replacement plan establish otherwise; they do not supersede [`art-direction.md`](art-direction.md) or [`overworld-art-framework.md`](overworld-art-framework.md).
